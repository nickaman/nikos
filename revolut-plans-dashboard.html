Cost-Benefit Crossover Analysis

Standard → Plus

If you exchange more than €1,000/month in foreign currency, the extra €2,000 fee-free allowance on Plus saves the 0.5% markup. Break-even point: ~€800/month extra FX beyond Standard's limit (i.e., €1,800 total). At that volume, savings of €4/month match the €3.99/month plan cost. Plus also adds priority support and basic travel medical insurance.

Plus → Premium

Premium costs €4 more/month than Plus but gives unlimited FX and €3,000/mo fee-free crypto trading. If you exceed €3,000/month FX, you save 0.5% on the excess. Break-even: €800/month FX above Plus's limit. Additionally, 3 lounge passes (~€30 each = €90/year value) and comprehensive travel insurance make this attractive for even moderate travelers.

Premium → Metal

Metal costs €6 more/month than Premium. Benefits: extra lounge pass (€30/yr), higher ATM limits (€400 extra/month), and 0.1-1% cashback. If you spend €6,000/month on card (1% outside Europe), cashback = €60/month -- the upgrade pays for itself many times over for heavy international spenders.

Metal → Ultra

Ultra costs €23-30 more/month than Metal. Key differentiator: unlimited crypto fee-free (saves 1.49% on all crypto trades beyond Metal's €3,000 limit). If you trade €2,000/month in crypto above Metal's limit, savings = €2,000 x 1.49% = €29.80/month, which nearly covers the full upgrade cost alone. Unlimited lounges, Priority Pass, personal manager, and 1% EU cashback (vs 0.1%) add further value.
